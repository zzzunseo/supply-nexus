# Map asset

`earth-dots.png` is a generated equirectangular texture with roughly 20,000
regularly spaced land dots. Geography is derived from Natural Earth 1:110m
Admin 0 Countries, public domain. Borders are cartographic reference only.

Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson

Natural Earth terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

The texture is packaged locally and does not fetch imagery at runtime.
It is an original code-generated visualization, not the AI-generated design preview.
