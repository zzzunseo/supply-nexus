# World geometry

`world_110m.json` downloaded from https://cdn.plot.ly/world_110m.json on 2026-10-26.
Plotly world geometry is based on Natural Earth public-domain map data.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Country outlines are for display. Sourced business location coordinates are kept
in data/verified_locations.json, with official address and coordinate references.
OpenStreetMap contributors provide building/campus geometry under ODbL:
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
Logical process outputs have no geographic display point.
